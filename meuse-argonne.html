1918

Pause on this number. The Meuse-Argonne Offensive was the largest operation the American army had ever fought, involving about 1.2 million U.S. soldiers. In 47 days, more than 26,000 Americans were killed, which makes it the deadliest single battle in American history, deadlier than Gettysburg, D-Day or the Battle of the Bulge. It was fought through dense forest, ravines and fortified German lines, and it continued right up to the armistice. Today the Meuse-Argonne American Cemetery in France holds more than 14,000 graves, the largest American military cemetery in Europe. The pattern of crosses on this slide is a small tribute to them.
