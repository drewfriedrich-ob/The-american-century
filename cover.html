Welcome to The American Century. Over the next hour we will travel from the battlefields of 1914 to the surface of the Moon in 1969. Each act opens with a hook and ends with a question that pulls us into the next one. Watch the small gold marker at the bottom of every slide: it is a time rail, and it moves as history moves. Four acts: the Great War, the years between the wars, World War II, and the Cold War, ending with the race to the Moon. Along the way, look for the slides titled Light in the Darkness. They tell the stories of ordinary people who showed extraordinary courage and faith when the world was at its worst.
