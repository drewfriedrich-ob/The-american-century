1898

Prologue

1898

Our story starts in 1898. The battleship Maine exploded in Havana harbor in February, and newspapers blamed Spain. The war that followed lasted roughly four months. Future Secretary of State John Hay called it “a splendid little war” in a letter to Theodore Roosevelt. America came out of it with an overseas empire: Puerto Rico, Guam and the Philippines, and it annexed Hawaii that same summer. Here is a detail worth remembering: the Panama Canal opened on August 15, 1914, just as Europe was sliding into the deadliest war it had ever known. America was rich, rising, and determined to stay out of Europe’s quarrels. That would not last.
