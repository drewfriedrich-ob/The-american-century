Act III · World War II

1941

This was truly a world war. The Axis powers were led by Germany, Italy and Japan, which formally allied in the Tripartite Pact of September 1940, joined by Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria and others; Finland fought the Soviet Union alongside Germany, and Thailand allied with Japan. Korea and Taiwan were Japanese colonies. The Allies were led by the “Big Three”: Britain, the Soviet Union after Hitler invaded it in June 1941, and the United States after Pearl Harbor, along with China, which had been fighting Japan since 1937, the Free French, and the nations of the British Commonwealth, including Canada, Australia, New Zealand, India and South Africa. Brazil sent troops to Italy; Mexico sent a fighter squadron to the Philippines. Spain, Portugal, Switzerland, Sweden and Ireland stayed neutral. The red dots mark turning points we will visit: Pearl Harbor, El Alamein, Stalingrad and D-Day.
