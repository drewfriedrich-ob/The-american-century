Here is the question that ties everything together: how did a young, mostly inward-looking republic become the most powerful nation on Earth, and what did that rise cost? We will answer it in four acts. The bar at the bottom maps the seventy-one years we will cover: a prologue that begins in 1898, the two world wars in red, the uneasy twenty years between them, and the long Cold War in blue. Our story ends in 1969, with Americans walking on the Moon. Keep that arc in mind as we go: from a nation just stepping onto the world stage to one that reached another world.
